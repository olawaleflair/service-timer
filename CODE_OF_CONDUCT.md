# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Church Timer Pro welcomes respectful participation from people of every background and experience level. Contributors, maintainers, and users should help keep project spaces constructive, safe, and focused on serving church media teams well.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual technical or community impact.
- Keep disagreements focused on ideas, evidence, and project scope.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish service plans, personal data, credentials, or vulnerability details.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and release readiness gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, or sustained disruption are not permitted. Publishing another person's private information or sensitive project information without permission is also prohibited.

## Enforcement

The project owner, [@olawaleflair](https://github.com/olawaleflair), is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this policy and has final authority over repository participation. Report conduct concerns privately to `omotosoolawale16@gmail.com`. Do not use a public issue when a report contains personal or sensitive details.

The owner may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily or permanently restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. Retaliation against anyone making a good-faith report is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project communication, and public interactions where someone is representing Church Timer Pro.
